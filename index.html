Our selection standard

A hospital is included only when it meets a clear, verifiable bar.

  • Official Grade IIIA (三甲) designation — China's top hospital tier
  • AND at least one of: Fudan overall tier A++ or above; a top-10 national specialty ranking; or current JCI accreditation
  • A public international department with English-language service and verifiable contact details
  • Excluded: hospitals that appear only in third-party marketing with no authoritative source
